# DB P92: coherent-cohort candidate, not admitted data

[Hoey's Nottingham thesis](https://eprints.nottingham.ac.uk/39989/1/Evaluation%20of%20High%20Temperature%20Performance%20of%20the%20Co-Cr-C%20Coated%20P92%20Steel%20Thomas%20Hoey%202016.pdf)
is a promising next source. Indexed full text describes one supplied pipe
section, its heat treatment, and uncoated creep, fatigue and dwell-fatigue
tests. Relevant locations: section 3.2.1; sections 3.4.2–3.4.4; tables
5.3–5.6 (PDF pages 177, 180, 181, 185).

Important: the indexed fatigue protocol goes from zero to positive strain
and back, not fully reversed. Its reported strain is therefore not the
amplitude of a symmetric cycle. Termination is fracture or a stress drop
exceeding 50%, unlike the trend-relative 10% criterion in the Imperial cohort.

The publisher file could not be retrieved during this audit: HTTPS reset,
redirected repository 404, and unavailable PDF screenshots. Consequently no
table transcription, model constants or numerical qualification is admitted
from this lead. Cached text is discovery evidence, not visual table verification.

Next: retrieve and hash the public original; inspect the listed pages and
waveforms; separate coated/uncoated observations; freeze calibration and
holdout roles. Retain mean strain, termination definition and stress-relaxation
history. Matching material provenance alone does not supply a dwell model.

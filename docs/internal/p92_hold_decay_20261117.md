# P92 hold decay versus across-cycle recovery

## Bounded experiment declared before fitting

Keep PR #197 open, with no release. Freeze the previous C1 candidate and vary
only the fast branch's existing isotropic time-recovery term. Use its initial
rate at R=0 and its target Qr as independent coordinates, rather than changing
both implicitly through one coefficient. This is the existing equation, not a
new constitutive law, clock, cycle-count factor or state reset:

`Rdot_time = (gamma_r1 + gamma_r2*exp(-r*p))*|Qr-R|**mr*sign(Qr-R)`.

For coordinates u, v set `Qr_new = Qr_seed*10**v` and multiply both gamma_r
coefficients by `10**(u-mr*v)`. At R=0 and any fixed accumulated strain p, the
signed time term is multiplied by `10**u`, independent of v. This is a
parameterization identity, not an assertion that real holds start at R=0.
The time term vanishes at R=Qr, but the accumulation term need not vanish;
Qr is therefore not necessarily the full equation's long-time equilibrium.

Prespecify u in [-1, 1] and v in [-1, 0]: rate scale 0.1–10 and negative target
magnitude 0.1–1 of the seed. A less-negative target preserves the existing
sufficient positive-strength bound. Coefficients may become numerically larger
through the change of coordinates; they are not evidence for stronger initial
softening unless u increases. Do not widen these bounds after fitting.

Only cycles 1–3 and existing weights are used, with 40 optimizer evaluations.
Keep both C1 values, all other kinematic terms and the slow branch fixed.
Freeze before later-cycle checks, retain Radau/BDF and switch-bracket checks,
and separately report early/late hold behavior rather than only aggregate cost.
Later inspected cycles remain development evidence, not independent validation.

## Literature basis and limits

[Zhang et al. (2020), equations 9–13](https://www.mdpi.com/2075-4701/10/3/307)
separate accumulated-strain-dependent static kinematic recovery from isotropic
recovery and its incomplete-recovery target. Their calibration sequence checks
hysteresis, cyclic peak evolution and hold relaxation separately. This
motivates separating observables and parameter effects here; the BAM 620 C
specimen is not their 600/650 C calibration material.

[Wang et al. (2021), institutional record](https://biblio.ugent.be/publication/8677638)
reports accumulated-inelastic-strain-dependent kinematic evolution and modified
isotropic evolution for P92 at 650 C. It motivates the next comparison if the
bounded existing-term experiment fails. It does not establish that adding
another history dependence will fit BAM or validate engineering life.

The first SLC hold follows compression and reversal. Keeping its inherited
backstress and R is essential: no fictitious virgin hold or thermal soak is
introduced. Time-recovery and strain-driven softening act together even within
a single hold; this test can discriminate candidate assumptions, not uniquely
identify real microstructural causes from three protocols.

## Numerical initialization finding

An initial boundary start `(0, 0)` terminated after two evaluations at
`(-1.02e-12, -2.00e-10)` with cost 0.015550383736, effectively unchanged from
0.015550383740. This was not accepted as evidence of an unresponsive mechanism.
A zero-bound start can create a tiny initial trust-region scale in the bounded
least-squares algorithm. The rerun used `(0, -0.05)` inside the same bounds,
with unchanged residuals and data, and now reports optimizer optimality.
No additional later-cycle observations were used to select parameters.

## Results and remaining failure

The interior-start fit converges in nine optimizer / 46 residual evaluations,
cost 0.01329958, optimality 1.25e-6. The initial-rate multiplier reaches its
upper bound (approximately 10); Qr becomes -74.642 MPa instead of -105.74 MPa.
The rate increase and shallower recovery target act together: do not attribute
the result to either coordinate alone. The slow branch and both C1 values are
unchanged. This is conditional calibration, not complete identifiability.

Frozen cycle-10 peak errors RF/SLR/SLC are -2.00%/+2.17%/-1.63%. Across **all**
development cycles 4–10, extrema RMS errors are 11.95/5.76/15.51 MPa and maximum
absolute peak-relative errors 4.48%/2.31%/1.63%. Valleys and holds are retained;
peak agreement alone does not establish acceptance. Three measured paths,
three nominal paths and their timing brackets complete ten cycles. Existing
Radau/BDF numerical refinement gates pass.

Late slopes use the first/last actual source samples in the last fifth of

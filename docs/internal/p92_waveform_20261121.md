# P92 fixed-card loading-waveform discriminator

## Prespecified comparison

Keep PR #197 open; no merge or release. Compare both previously frozen
time-shape and kinematic-shape cards, without fitting any parameter. The latter
was not promoted as a better card. Use all three BAM protocols, cycles 1–10,
with identical switches, observations, residual weights and initial states.
Cycles 4–10 remain inspected development checks, not independent validation.
Replay lower/upper recorded switch brackets with the same waveform construction
as a separate sensitivity, retaining common unambiguous observation identities.
For the first SLC cycle, also replace only the initial compression ramp or only
the reversal-to-zero ramp. Preserve the intervening history; these nonlinear
effects are not assumed additive.

The first question is **ramp shape**, not an unrestricted controller fit.
For each strain-controlled ramp, linearly interpolate every recorded strain
sample and subtract the chord through its measured endpoints. Add that
zero-endpoint deviation to the existing predicted-start/declared-end ramp.
Integrate separately at every slope discontinuity, carrying all ten state
components. Keep peak-strain dwells and constant predicted-stress SLC holds
unchanged. Measured hold strain remains a response, never an input.

This isolates waveform shape while preserving the existing endpoint control
contract. It is not exact absolute controller replay: after an SLC hold,
predicted strain can differ from measurement. Record the initial/final strain
offset for every shaped ramp. Do not reset strain, stress, backstress or
softening history to hide this mismatch. This test cannot rule out all control
model errors or uncertain experimental prehistory.

A separate first-cycle SLC check replays the complete recorded strain prefix,
including the compression dwell's measured drift, up to the first stress hold.
This is a measured-input sensitivity, not proof of the controller command.
It never imposes the measured creep response after stress control begins.
Independent integration verifies this prefix as well.

## Conditional next experiment, declared before evaluation

If the fixed-card comparison leaves a substantial stress mismatch, inspect
the existing second backstress before adding a memory variable. With the
time-shape card frozen, vary each branch's a2/C2 at fixed C2*a2, and separately
C2 at fixed a2, by +/-0.1 log10 units. Use only cycles 1–3 of all three
protocols with measured ramp shape. Report individual hold histories,
directional residual sensitivity, nonlinearity and singular values. This is
a four-direction diagnostic, not an optimizer or an accepted replacement card.
The rationale is to separate reversal curvature from initial signed hardening;
no cycle-index term or state reset is introduced. Later cycles remain untouched
by this diagnostic. Failure of control admissibility stops the experiment.

[The Walker-based hybrid-control study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijmecsci.2022.107369)
supports examining hardening and dynamic recovery across loading controls.
Only its abstract was available here; its equations and quantitative accuracy
are not being reproduced or claimed. The conditional test uses our existing
explicitly documented hypothesis, not a purported implementation of that paper.

The completed sensitivity has singular values 0.4353, 0.1341, 0.02794 and
0.002392: the slow shape direction is especially weak. The next bounded fit
therefore varies only the two fast second-backstress coordinates jointly,
each within +/-0.3 log10 units, at most 25 optimizer evaluations. All other
parameters, early-cycle samples and objective scales stay fixed. This is
declared before fitting; no widening after an active bound. Save a frozen
card before any ten-cycle replay. Neither convergence nor improved aggregate
cost constitutes response admission.

## Numerical and scientific gates

- Linear-shape elastic and nonlinear limits reproduce the original integrator.
- Identical-shape knot subdivision and independent Radau/BDF integration retain
  stress-like endpoint agreement below 1e-4 MPa and strain-like agreement below
  1e-8. These are numerical gates, not experimental acceptance tolerances.
- Holds never acquire prescribed strain; later-cycle data cannot enter an
  earlier window, including duplicate timestamps outside that window.
- Report ramp stress RMS, hold entry stress, early/late hold strain secants,
  cycle peaks/valleys, all parameter/source identities and completed cycles.
- A smaller aggregate cost is not sufficient. Compare the first SLC inherited
  stress bias and subsequent creep rates separately. Do not widen fit bounds.

If shape cannot explain the bias, retain this negative result and investigate
reverse-loading material response, not another input correction. Joint early
identification comes only after a discriminating mechanism test; freeze before
later-cycle replay. Reserve independent data for genuine validation.

## Sources and limitations

The [BAM data article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2023.109333) distinguishes
strain-controlled RF/SLR from mixed-control SLC. These sampled histories are
not complete controller logs. The files are admitted by their previously
recorded checksums; personal header metadata is neither needed nor copied.

[Zhang et al.](https://www.mdpi.com/2075-4701/10/3/307) motivate separate
hysteresis, softening and relaxation checks, not peak-only selection. Their
600/650 C parameters are not a validated BAM 620 C material card.

A directly relevant [2025 institutional record](https://opus.hs-offenburg.de/11632)
describes non-unified modeling of P92 service-like tests. Its abstract supports
investigating separate mechanisms, but the record marks the chapter closed
access. No unavailable equations, parameter table or claimed performance are
used as implementation or validation evidence.
The separately open FVWHT contribution from these authors was already reviewed
in [the source admission audit](p92_external_source_admission_20261106.md).
Reuse that evidence; do not conflate its 600 C cohort with BAM 620 C.

## Fixed-card results

Both frozen cards completed all three protocols for ten continuous cycles.
Independent Radau/BDF endpoint stress differences are below 1.2e-6 MPa;
strain-state differences are below 1.5e-10. Same-waveform knot refinement
also passes. The complete recorded pre-stress-hold prefix independently
passes both integrators.

| Frozen card | First SLC stress, linear | Ramp shape | Full recorded strain prefix | Observed |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Time-shape | 138.74 MPa | 138.02 MPa | 137.98 MPa | 121.70 MPa |
| Kinematic-shape | 143.06 MPa | 142.22 MPa | 142.23 MPa | 121.70 MPa |

Shape improves aggregate SLC cost but worsens RF and SLR cost for both cards.
Neither card passes the prospective response screen. For the time-shape
card, first-hold strain at the final sample changes from 0.0009057 to
0.0008893 (observed 0.0010600), while its early creep secant improves.
This is a tradeoff, not material acceptance. Ten late SLC secants have
insufficient recorded resolution for the screen and remain unresolved.

The loading-discrimination milestone is achieved for these measured inputs:
reversal waveform approximation is not the dominant explanation of the first
hold's 16–21 MPa inherited-stress bias. This does not remove experimental
prehistory or all controller uncertainty from the error budget.

Numerical audit found an inconsistent domain guard: it tolerated adjacent
accumulated-strain changes of 1e-10 but rejected any endpoint decrease, even
below the ODE error scale. The replacement checks cumulative drawdown against
the actual atol + rtol*|p| scale, rejects negative final accumulated strain,
and never clips or resets state. Separate fixed scientific/numerical gates
are unchanged. Regressions reject cumulative drift beyond that scale.
An initial restricted-environment test execution ended with signal 15;
the same targeted set subsequently completed in the normal numerical
environment (206 tests at that checkpoint). It was not recorded as a pass.

## Frozen conditional identification result

The two-coordinate fit converged in seven optimizer evaluations (35 residual
evaluations), with neither bound active. Log10 coordinates are -0.04210 and
+0.07602; its two sensitivity singular values are 0.4154 and 0.2174. Early
aggregate cost falls from 0.0140708 to 0.0135176, about 3.9%.

After freezing, all three ten-cycle paths and numerical checks complete, but
all three response screens still fail. Cycle-10 peak errors are +0.33% RF,
+5.15% SLR and +3.76% SLC. The first SLC hold stress improves only to 136.42
MPa, versus 121.70 MPa; final first-hold strain worsens to 0.0007948 versus
0.0010600. At cycle 10 the early creep secant remains 4.621e-5/s versus
3.186e-5/s. This candidate is **not promoted**. More freedom in this same
kinematic pair is not the next default action.

The next discriminator must separate inherited reversal stress, initial hold
creep and cross-cycle evolution while preserving shared stress and continuous
history. Compare mechanism contributions at these events before introducing
another recovery-memory law. Do not compensate first-hold underprediction by
increasing a global creep rate that worsens later early-hold excess. A new law
requires accessible equations, bounded state evolution, independent numerical
checks and a new prospectively fixed early-data identification experiment.

The frozen card is `evidence/creep/2026-11-21-reversal-fit.json`; the full
later-cycle comparison is `evidence/creep/2026-11-21-reversal-frozen.json`.
Neither uses cycles 4–10 in identification or treats them as blind holdouts.

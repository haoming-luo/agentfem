# P92 conditional flow-shape experiment

## Evidence motivating the test

The frozen waveform audit excludes ramp shape as the dominant first-hold
stress-error source. Signed branch-flow integration on the same trajectory
shows that the nominal fast branch supplies about 93% of first-hold strain
and 97% at cycle 10. Its label therefore does not imply a short-time-only role.
These are model contributions, not measured mechanisms or damage fractions.
Integrated signed flow is checked against the strain/stress increment, without
equating signed strain to accumulated absolute strain.

The previous two-coordinate backstress fit improves aggregate early cost only
3.9% and worsens first-hold strain. It is not the new seed: retain the earlier
time-shape card for the next independent conditional experiment.

## Prospective controls

For the fast branch write flow magnitude as q_ref*(overstress/120 MPa)^n.
Here overstress excludes backstress and current strength; 120 MPa is not
the applied hold stress. First compare exponents 4, 5 and the frozen 6 at
unchanged q_ref, all other parameters and histories fixed. This changes the
low/high-overstress rate ratio without arbitrary cycle-dependent corrections.

Then identify only n and log10(q_ref/q_ref_seed), bounded to n in [3, 8]
and log10 rate scale in [-0.5, 0.5], at most 25 optimizer evaluations.
Use the same measured-shape inputs, residual weights and cycles 1–3 of all
three protocols. No widened bounds, state resets or later-cycle fitting.
Freeze before ten-cycle numerical, switch-bracket and response checks.
If an improved scalar cost trades away first-hold creep or other protocols,
do not promote the candidate.

This is a sensitivity of the existing power-law hypothesis, not a new
published parameter set or an inferred microscopic mechanism. The public
[two-rate discussion](https://opus.hs-offenburg.de/files/11628/SeifertSchlayerMaier_PlastCrpFat_FVWHT_2025.pdf)
organizes branches by response time rather than assuming that a preset high
exponent automatically separates plasticity from creep. Its cohort differs
from BAM; it does not supply transferable numerical parameters here.

PR #197 remains open. No production material admission, merge or release.

## Loading convention cross-check

The [BAM experimental-design description](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10319173/)
specifies the SLC hold at sigma(epsilon=0), not an externally imposed universal
120 MPa setpoint. Consequently, replacing inherited predicted stress by the
observed hold stress would conceal a constitutive loading-path error. Keep the
continuous switch and evaluate the observed hold stress as an output. Recorded
stress drift and sampling uncertainty remain separately visible.

## Audit checkpoint

The signed-flow audit closes all ten SLC hold strain increments to below
2.3e-15. The fast-branch fractions at cycles 1, 3 and 10 are 0.9315, 0.9707
and 0.9717. These are conditional model diagnostics only.

The exponent/rate fit converges without active bounds at n=4.3548 and a
reference-rate scale of 10^0.006218. Early cost falls to 0.0115028 (18.3%),
but frozen cycle-10 SLC peak error becomes -10.14%; the first hold stress is
141.07 MPa and cycle-10 early creep is 6.226e-5/s versus 3.186e-5/s observed.
All three response screens fail despite independent numerical agreement.
This candidate is not promoted.

Before considering a new history variable, perform one bounded joint check
of the two already tested coordinate pairs. Start from the original time-shape
card, use the same four individual bounds and the same cycles 1–3, at most
25 optimizer evaluations. This tests interaction between identified directions,
not new constitutive terms or a changed objective. Freeze before later
replay. If the tradeoff remains, stop expanding this conditional search and
design a distinct history-evolution hypothesis with its own validation gates.

The joint fit converged in eight optimizer evaluations (72 residual calls).
Early cost fell to 0.0088801, about 36.9% below the frozen time-shape baseline,
but n reached the lower bound of 3. The frozen cycle-10 peak errors are
+5.83% RF, +10.44% SLR and -0.35% SLC. First SLC hold stress is 133.38 MPa
versus 121.70 MPa; its early secant is 5.581e-5/s versus 3.568e-5/s, and
its late secant is 4.814e-8/s versus 1.134e-7/s. All response screens fail.
Independent integration and knot refinement pass. This is not a successful
material validation despite the attractive SLC peak result.

The declared conditional search is closed without promoting a candidate.
The next [scalar reference design](p92_two_rate_reference_design_20261121.md)
separates component recovery times and supplies explicit mechanical accounting.
It must first pass numerical and thermodynamic checks, and then get its own
calibration identity. It is not silently substituted into any old parameter card.
